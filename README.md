# Venesse | Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

### End-to-End Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Portfolio Project

**Technologies:** SQL Server | T-SQL | Power BI | DAX | Python | Pandas

## Project Overview

Venesse is an end-to-end retail analytics portfolio project designed to transform transactional business data into actionable insights.

The project covers data cleaning, relational database modeling, SQL analysis, Power BI reporting, customer behavior, marketing performance, product profitability, customer satisfaction, and financial performance.

The objective is to support data-driven decisions across sales, customer retention, marketing investment, product strategy, and operating expenses.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Clean and validate transactional data using SQL Server.
2. **Data Modeling:** Build relational tables and analytical views.
3. **Business Analysis:** Develop SQL queries to investigate business performance.
4. **Power BI:** Create analytical dashboards using DAX measures and interactive visualizations.
5. **Python:** Explore customer feedback and data analysis using Pandas.
6. **Validation:** Reconcile financial metrics and business KPIs against SQL results.

## Key Business Questions

- How is revenue performing compared with the previous year?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- How effectively are customers being retained?
- Which marketing campaigns generate the strongest financial returns?
- Which products and categories drive profitability?
- What are customers saying about their experience?
- How do operating expenses affect overall business profitability?

## Dashboard Analysis

### Sales Performance
Revenue trends, gross profit, profit margins, year-over-year comparisons, and leading products.

### Customer Analytics
Customer segmentation, retention, repeat purchasing, top customer contributions, and revenue at risk.

### Marketing Campaign Performance
Campaign-attributed revenue, marketing spending, ROAS, conversion rates, and profitability.

### Product Performance & Profitability
Product and category profitability, regional comparisons, and changes in selling prices and costs.

### Customer Feedback & Satisfaction
Customer ratings, positive and negative feedback, regional satisfaction, and complaint classification.

### Operating Expenses & Profitability
Operating expense analysis, campaign costs, operating profit, operating margins, and year-over-year financial performance.

## Selected Business Findings

**January–September 2026**

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | $938,352.11 |
| Gross Profit | $229,097.40 |
| Operating Expenses | $272,445.27 |
| Campaign Costs | $16,076.30 |
| Operating Profit | -$59,424.17 |
| Operating Margin | -6.33% |

Compared with January–September 2025, revenue increased, and the operating loss decreased by approximately 49%.

Despite improving financial performance, the business remained unprofitable during the analyzed period.

*Financial note: Campaign costs are treated as additional to operating expenses in the current model. Potential overlap with marketing expense categories has not been independently ruled out.*

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate opportunities to improve gross margins on high-performing products.
- Prioritize marketing campaigns based on profitability rather than revenue alone.
- Strengthen customer retention strategies using purchasing behavior and revenue-at-risk analysis.
- Introduce structured customer feedback collection to identify specific causes of dissatisfaction.
- Review major operating expense categories to improve financial sustainability.
